# Optional author style profile

Load this profile only when requested or established by project instructions. It
distills preferences described in the user's supplied GPT 6 Astra Pro guidance.
The earlier manuscript and the separate skill mentioned there were not supplied
as source artifacts here; no manuscript-specific finding is independently verified.
A current user-provided writing sample takes priority over this profile.

Use concrete research stakes and explicit mechanisms where the evidence supports
them. Explain the behavior behind an important technical concept, then use its
established name consistently. Anticipate the reader's likely confusion without
adding repetitive signposts or explaining every familiar term.

Make transitions identify what previous evidence accomplished and why the next
question follows. Make numerical comparisons interpretable, including their sign,
units, baseline, and operational meaning. Keep qualifications and exceptions close
to the claims they limit. Distinguish practical guidance from retrospective evidence.

For economics and marketing audiences, explain decision relevance when it belongs
to the research question. Favor connected prose when that helps the argument, but
retain useful lists and adapt to another genre when requested. Do not imitate
particular paragraph lengths, repeated phrases, or managerial framing mechanically.

This profile adjusts presentation, not scientific scope. It does not authorize new
anecdotes, personality, business claims, or embellishment of novelty.
